How Staten Island Voted in the 2024 Presidential Election

Donald Trump won Staten Island in the 2024 presidential election, with much of the borough voting Republican. However, election district results show a different pattern on the North Shore, where Kamala Harris won several districts. The map below shows how voting varied across Staten Island.

While Trump won most election districts on Staten Island, Harris saw stronger support in parts of the North Shore. The results show how voting patterns can differ across neighborhoods, even within a borough that largely supported one candidate.
